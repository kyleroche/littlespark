Play all three notes to finish the lesson.

This is a connection visualization and a browser instrument. Real-board mode accepts keyboard events; it cannot identify your board or inspect physical wiring.

No note? Check these things.

Click Sound on, then Focus piano. Keep this tab active. Hold the bare metal end of the EARTH clip while touching a fruit. Check each clip grips the metal pad and fruit. Keep fruits and clip ends from touching one another. Try the keyboard’s Space, Left and Up keys: if those play, check the board’s USB cable and connections. In virtual practice, switch virtual EARTH on. Foil pads can replace fruit.

Use a dry surface and keep food away from the computer. Have a grown-up help with USB and clips. Use only the Makey Makey’s USB connection for this activity.
