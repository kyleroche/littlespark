How we use it—and what we don’t do

We process family data only to provide and secure the learning service, save progress, respond to parents and carry out deletion. We do not sell, rent, trade or share it for targeted advertising. We do not train or fine-tune AI models on family data. There are no advertising or product-analytics trackers, public profiles, public child posts, or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Where GDPR applies, we rely on providing the service requested by the parent for parent-account operations, parent/guardian authorization and consent for optional child profiles and cloud progress, and legitimate interests for proportionate account security and abuse prevention. You can withdraw permission by deleting a child or family; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. The account service needs the information above; you can read the public guides without an account.

Parents: download, correct or delete

  1. Sign in using a new parent email link and open Family.
  2. Open Your family’s data, then Download family data for a machine-readable JSON copy of your account, child profiles and saved lesson records. Keep that file private.
  3. To correct a nickname or icon, use the child’s Edit profile form.
  4. To remove one child, choose Edit profile → Delete this child profile and type the exact nickname. To remove everyone, choose Delete the whole family account, enter your parent email and type DELETE.

Exports and deletions require a parent email sign-in from the last 15 minutes. Children cannot export or delete the family or access siblings. Deletion is permanent: it removes the relevant profiles, lesson records, sessions and child links from the active database. Deleting the family also removes the parent account and its login link. All affected devices must sign in again; old child links cannot restore the deleted profile.

Deletion clears the affected cached profile records in the browser performing it. Other devices may retain local copies until their site data is cleared; we cannot remotely erase browser storage or copies you downloaded. Use your browser’s clear-site-data control on those devices. New accounts do not inherit deleted IDs or progress.

For email correction, access help, restriction, objection, deletion assistance or other rights requests, email the privacy contact above. Where GDPR applies, we respond within one month, subject to lawful extensions, and explain any refusal. We may verify control of the parent email to protect your family; please do not send identity documents unless a necessary, secure verification process is agreed. You can complain to your local data protection authority, including in the EEA or UK.

How long we keep information

  • Family accounts and progress: while the parent keeps the family account open, to preserve lessons across visits and devices, until the parent deletes the child or family. We do not automatically delete inactive family accounts. Parents can export or delete at any time. We review the continued necessity of retained child data periodically; an open account is not permission to keep data for unrelated purposes.
  • Login links: work once and expire after five minutes. Expired link records and rate-limit rows are removed by the daily cleanup after a one-day buffer, normally within two days of expiry.
  • Sessions: parent sessions expire after 30 days and child sessions after seven days; expired records are cleaned daily. Signing out or revoking a child ends access sooner.
  • Device storage: local lesson/practice copies and layout preferences stay on that browser until cleared. They do not automatically disappear when the server retention period ends.
  • Deletion receipts: a minimal internal ID, record type and deletion time are kept for 35 days to avoid restoring deleted accounts from backups. No email, nickname or lesson content is stored in the receipt.
  • Provider records: active-database deletion does not instantly purge provider backups, delivery records or security logs. Neon retains recovery history according to its configured restore window; restored data must have deletion requests reapplied before it is used. Resend documents 30-day message/log retention on standard plans, with seven-day backups. Hosting security and delivery records follow provider schedules and applicable legal requirements. Contact us for assistance with records that require a provider request.

The daily cleanup removes expired security credentials, not family accounts or progress. Any legally required exception to deletion must be documented and limited to that purpose.

Cookies and browser storage

The ls_session cookie keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only, host-only, SameSite=Lax and secure over production HTTPS. Browser storage saves layout preferences, parent practice and child progress caches for recovery/sync. These are used for the service you request, not cross-site tracking. Clearing them signs you out and removes local copies. Fonts and illustrations are served by Little Spark rather than Google Fonts. External links open separate services under their own policies.
